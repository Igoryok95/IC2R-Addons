# Built-in imagegen prompts for 1.2.0

## Tool atlas

Create one production pixel-art sprite atlas for a Minecraft IndustrialCraft addon. Match IC2R
orange / amber handle grips, charcoal outlines, metallic silver drill bits and upper-left highlights,
while retaining the original addon's blue upgrade details and diagonal silhouettes. Exactly two
columns and two rows, transparent RGBA, no labels or background. Top-left: advanced diamond drill,
orange motor grip, blue collar and cyan diamond bit. Top-right: advanced iridium drill, reinforced
orange grip, blue energy detail and silver-white stepped bit. Bottom-left: advanced chainsaw,
orange grip, blue collar and silver toothed blade. Bottom-right: powered multi-tool wrench,
orange / graphite insulated handle, blue indicator and silver open jaw. Each cell represents a
native 32x32 sprite, hard pixel edges, no antialiasing or gradients, with transparent padding.
Reference: `1.2.0-reference.png`. Output: `1.2.0-tools-generated.png`.

## Equipment atlas

Edit the single 4x4 Minecraft inventory sprite atlas. Keep exactly the original columns, rows,
cell order, identity, silhouette and occupancy. Upgrade original 16x16 addon art into crisp native
32x32 pixels per cell with restrained IndustrialCraft 2 Refactored steel highlights, graphite
shadows and blue energy detail. Transparent background, no text / borders / shadows / antialiasing.
Row 1: dark solar nano helmet, dark advanced nano chestplate with wing-like shoulders, dark nano
leggings, dark nano boots. Row 2: white hybrid helmet, white ultimate helmet, white gravitational
chestplate with wing-like shoulders, white quantum leggings. Row 3: white quantum boots, white
advanced electric jetpack with twin blue thrusters, gray advanced lappack with blue cells, steel
ultimate lappack with blue / red cells. Row 4: original red / graphite Vajra, blue horseshoe magnet
with red / silver pole tips, pale steel circular voider with red / yellow center, cyan relocator
with teal star center. Keep angular original flat Minecraft silhouettes; all armor energy details
must remain blue / cyan, not green. Modest 32-pixel detail, no shiny 3D RPG rendering.
Reference: `1.2.0-equipment-reference.png`. Output: `1.2.0-equipment-generated.png`.
